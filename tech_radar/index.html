    About the Tech Radar¶

    The tech radar is a visual tool that categorizes technologies into four rings:

    • ADOPT: Technologies we have high confidence in and recommend for widespread use
    • TRIAL: Technologies worth pursuing with known success cases but some risk
    • ASSESS: Promising technologies worth exploring with higher risks
    • HOLD: Technologies to proceed with caution or avoid for new projects

    Technologies are also organized into four quadrants:

    • Languages & Frameworks: Programming languages and development frameworks
    • Tools: Development and operational tools
    • Platforms: Infrastructure and platform technologies
    • Techniques: Methods, processes, and architectural patterns

    The tech radar is reviewed and updated every semester (twice a year). Each update may promote, demote, or introduce technologies based on our practical experience and the broader ecosystem's evolution.

    Technologies are evaluated based on criteria such as:

    • Maturity: How established and stable the technology is
    • Adoption: The level of adoption in the industry and within our projects
    • Community Support: The strength and activity of the community around the technology
    • Alignment with Goals: How well the technology aligns with our project goals and values

    The radar visualization is based on the Zalando Tech Radar implementation.
