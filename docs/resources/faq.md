# Frequently Asked Questions

## Where the DoKa Seca name come from?

The name "DoKa Seca" is a playful Portuguese phrase where "DoKa" incorporates the "K" from Kubernetes (representing the
containerized orchestration at the heart of this project), and "Seca" means "dry" - drawing inspiration from the concept
of a **dry dock**. Just as ships are built, repaired, and maintained in dry docks - controlled, isolated environments
where all the necessary infrastructure and tooling are readily available - DoKa Seca provides a "dry dock" for Kubernetes
platforms. It creates an isolated, controlled environment where entire cloud-native platforms can be rapidly assembled,
configured, and tested before being deployed to production waters.
